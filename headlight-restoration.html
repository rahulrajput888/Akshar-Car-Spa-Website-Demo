What's Included

See Clearly. Drive Safe.

Oxidised, yellow headlights cut your night visibility. We sand, buff and seal them back to crystal-clear brightness.

  • Headlight condition & crack inspection
  • Masking of surrounding paint & trim
  • Multi-stage wet sanding of oxidation
  • Machine buffing to optical clarity
  • UV sealant to slow future yellowing
  • Both headlights + fog-lamp lenses
  • Before/after check with you
