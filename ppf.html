What is PPF?

An Invisible Shield for Your Factory Paint

PPF is a transparent, ultra-tough urethane film applied over your car's paint. It absorbs stone chips, shrugs off scratches (self-healing with heat), blocks UV fading and resists bird droppings & road grime — while keeping 100% of your original colour and gloss.

  • Heals light scratches automatically in sunlight
  • Hydrophobic top-coat — water & dirt slide off
  • Non-invasive & removable — preserves resale value
  • Safe for original paint & company warranty

Which One Do You Need?
